# Blog post drafts (unpublished)

The posts in this folder are unpublished drafts. They are not the posts on
ferni.ai: the published posts live in `apps/website/ferni-website/src/blog/`,
and most of them were rewritten there, so these drafts differ from what is
live.

**`10-stories-from-the-2am-hour.md` describes invented people.** An earlier
version presented its stories as real conversations with real people who gave
permission. They are composites, and the draft now says so. Keep that
disclaimer in any version that is published.
