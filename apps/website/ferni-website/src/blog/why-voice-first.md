Ever notice how different it feels to type a message versus say it out loud?

When you type, you edit. You backspace. You second-guess. But when you talk? Thoughts flow. Ideas connect. You discover what you actually think by hearing yourself say it.

That's why Ferni is voice-first.

## Talking Is How We Think

There's a reason you call a friend instead of texting when something's really on your mind. Saying it out loud to someone who's listening feels different. When you talk it through:

- You hear what you actually feel, sometimes for the first time
- One thought leads to the next, and you notice connections you'd have typed right past
- You stop polishing. There's no time to craft the "right" answer, so the real one comes out

Ferni is made for this kind of thinking out loud. No typing. Just a conversation, like the ones you'd have with a good friend.

## Response Time Matters

Real conversations don't have loading spinners.

We obsess over latency at Ferni. Today a reply takes around two seconds, which is slower than a person and slower than we want. Closing that gap is some of the most important work we do.

This isn't just a nice feature. It changes the entire experience. When responses come fast, your train of thought stays intact. You think more clearly because you're not waiting.

## The Future of Personal AI

Text-based AI is amazing for tasks—writing emails, summarizing documents, generating code.

But for personal growth? For working through decisions? For having someone to talk to?
