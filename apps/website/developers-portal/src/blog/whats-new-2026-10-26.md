## Smoother mobile interactions

Android devices and phone browsers use the back gesture to go back in browser history. Previously, when people opened any of the 37 panels or sheets, pressing back left the web app completely instead of closing the panel. In [#2202](https://github.com/ferni-ai/voiceai/pull/2202), we added history management for open overlays. Now, back closes the top panel or sheet while keeping people inside the app.

We also landed [#2358](https://github.com/ferni-ai/voiceai/pull/2358) to raise the touch target floor on touch screens to 48 px. On mobile screens, an audit measured 69 targets under 48 px, with some links and buttons as small as 14 px tall. Now, buttons, close targets, and links offer at least a 48 px touch area under coarse pointers, without altering desktop layouts.

## Visual fixes and dark themes

Night mode on the home page previously created low contrast for people whose operating system was set to dark mode. In [#2443](https://github.com/ferni-ai/voiceai/pull/2443), we updated styles so card titles, descriptions, and text keep daytime dark mode contrast ratios like 13.4:1 down to 6.2:1. Relatedly, [#2706](https://github.com/ferni-ai/voiceai/pull/2706) replaced 31 hard-coded colours across 15 stylesheets with theme roles, updating 76 elements across 34 pages in dark and night modes.

We also landed [#2464](https://github.com/ferni-ai/voiceai/pull/2464) to ensure the avatar's eyelids take the page colour. By removing a dead face-shade rule, we stop platform visual drift across themes like zen and midnight.

On smaller screens at 1280x800, a failed call notice previously pushed control buttons off screen. In [#2136](https://github.com/ferni-ai/voiceai/pull/2136), the call notice now floats above controls on a solid surface. This work also adds clear empty and error states with a retry option for This Year Together.

## Accessibility and panel updates

In [#2155](https://github.com/ferni-ai/voiceai/pull/2155), we addressed accessibility findings across menu panels. We updated text colours for better contrast, made regions keyboard-scrollable, updated icons, and deduplicated identical GET requests in flight so retries spread out evenly when handling a 502 error.

In [#2157](https://github.com/ferni-ai/voiceai/pull/2157), we updated panel titles so each panel matches its menu item name, such as The Chronicle and When We've Talked. We also replaced 42 em dashes in en-US and 44 in en-GB with commas, colons, or full stops.

Finally, [#2883](https://github.com/ferni-ai/voiceai/pull/2883) landed a guard test for all 11 locales to ensure all 63 keys for the How You're Feeling panel exist and stay translated.
